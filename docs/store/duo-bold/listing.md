## Name

Duo Bold

## Summary

Big stacked time, nothing else.

## Description

Hours over minutes in a heavy geometric type, white on black. The date sits under the minutes. That is the whole face.

Always-on shows the same digits at hairline weight so the screen stays quiet and inside Garmin's burn-in limits.

No settings, no permissions, no data leaves the watch.

Built for the Forerunner 965. Other AMOLED watches follow.
