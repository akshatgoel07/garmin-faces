## Name

Trio Chrono

## Summary

A chronograph dial with a turning seconds disc.

## Description

Twelve numbered cells round the rim, three subdials, and a green seconds disc that turns under a yellow pointer. A blue hour hand, a long minute hand, and the date in a pill.

The subdials work: steps and battery on the left, local time and UTC on a 24 hour scale on the right, seconds at the bottom.

Black or white dial, picked in the settings.

Always-on keeps the same dial as fine lines, well inside Garmin's burn-in limits.

No permissions, no data leaves the watch.

Built for the Forerunner 965. Other AMOLED watches follow.
